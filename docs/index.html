How it works

One phone at the gate, one badge per person.

The phone's role follows what it holds: the employee list makes it the gate, a saved badge makes it an employee's phone.

01

Import the list

On the gate phone, import a CSV with id, name and department columns. Any spreadsheet saved as CSV works.

02

Hand out badges

Each employee scans their badge once from the gate phone, and the app opens on it from then on. Badges can also be shared and printed.

03

Scan at the door

The guard scans a badge, checks the name, and records it. The phone picks in or out by itself.

Features

Small, on purpose.

The guard has one job: scan badges. HR gets the records as a spreadsheet.

In or out, decided for you

A clock-in from the last 16 hours means this scan is a clock-out. Night shifts and forgotten clock-outs just work, and the guard can switch it.

Only people on the list

Re-import the list and anyone left out is refused at the gate. Their past records keep their names.

CSV export for HR

Share or save the attendance as CSV, with the raw timestamp plus date and time columns, ready for Excel.

Works offline

No server, no account and no internet. Everything stays on the gate phone.

The employee list

A spreadsheet is enough.

Column order and letter case don't matter, and files saved with semicolons work too. Bad lines are skipped and listed by number.
